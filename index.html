This section provides a high-level overview of the key advice from the source material. Mindset & Approach

  • Presence of Mind is Key: The interview values your on-the-spot thinking and approach over rote memorization.
  • Presentation Matters: How you structure and deliver your answers is as important as the content itself.
  • Rural Challenges: Be aware of the primary challenge in rural areas: a lack of resources (supplies, specialists, support).
  • Stay Calm: The experience is designed to be engaging. Don't take unnecessary stress.

What NOT to Do

  • ✗ Don't arrive late or appear unorganized.
  • ✗ Don't bad-mouth past employers or lie about qualifications.
  • ✗ Don't give one-word answers or interrupt the interviewer.
  • ✗ Don't forget to turn off your cell phone.

Role Comparison: PHC vs. CHC/SDH

Understanding the differences between working at a Primary Health Centre (PHC) and a Community Health Centre (CHC) or Sub-District Hospital (SDH) is crucial. This section presents a side-by-side comparison of the pros and cons for each setting, helping you form an informed perspective on your potential work environment.

PHC (Primary Health Centre)

Pros

  • Regular hours (9 to 6 job)
  • No emergency, MLC, or PM duties
  • Lower patient load
  • Weekends and festival days off

Cons

  • Limited clinical exposure
  • Heavy focus on statistical/program work
  • Requires staying in a village or commuting
  • May feel less like a "doctor" due to less clinical work

CHC / SDH (Community Health Centre)

Pros

  • Maintains clinical touch and skills
  • Can settle with family in a town/city
  • Duty can be managed with other doctors
  • Feel more like a practicing doctor

Cons

  • MLC and PM duties are required
  • Handles emergency cases
  • No fixed Sunday/festival holidays
  • Must attend court for MLC cases, slight political pressure
  • Can be hectic if understaffed

Interview Day Guide & Procedure

This section outlines the step-by-step process for your interview day, from arrival to the viva itself. Knowing the logistics beforehand can help reduce anxiety and ensure you are well-prepared. Pay close attention to the rules regarding personal items and identification to ensure a smooth process.

Arrival & Check-in

  • Arrival Time: Reach by 8:15 AM. Entry begins at 8:30 AM.
  • Documents: Show your call letter at the gate. Bring your application form, all required xerox copies, and a pen.

Pre-Interview Process

  • The staff will explain the document verification order.
  • You will be asked to choose a unique ID and a panel number via a draw (chits). Your turn is not based on your roll number.

The Viva (Interview)

  • Anonymity: You are not allowed to say your name. Use the unique code you were given.
  • No Personal Items: Watch, pen, documents, etc., are not allowed inside the interview room.
  • Structure: The interview is divided into verification and viva. There are 3 panels with 4 examiners each. Each examiner asks questions for 5-6 minutes.
  • Introduction: Be prepared to state when you completed your UG and what your current job is.
  • Duration: The interview lasts approximately 20 minutes per candidate.
